Terminology.

  • Type-Token Ratio (TTR): Unique Words ÷ Total Words.
    (Measures vocabulary richness)
  • Number of Hedges: Count of occurrences of hedge words/phrases from a predefined list.
  • Number of Boosters: Count of occurrences of booster words/phrases from a predefined list.
  • Frequency of Personal Pronouns: Count of occurrences of predefined personal pronouns.
  • Punctuation Frequency: Total punctuation count with a breakdown in brackets (Period, Comma, Exclamation, Question, Semicolon, Colon).
  • Average Sentence Length: Total Number of Words ÷ Number of Sentences.
  • Passive Voice Usage (%): (Number of Sentences with Passive Voice ÷ Total Number of Sentences) × 100.
  • Total Characters/Total Number of Words: Total characters (excluding spaces) ÷ Total Words.
  • Consonants/Vowels: Total number of predefined consonants ÷ Total number of predefined vowels.
  • Coleman-Liau Index: CLI = 0.0588 × L − 0.296 × S − 15.8, where L is the average number of letters per 100 words and S is the average number of sentences per 100 words.
    (Indicates the U.S. grade level needed to understand the text)
  • Figures of Speech – Similes Count: Count of occurrences detected using simile patterns.
  • Figures of Speech – Repetition Count: Count of instances where the same word appears consecutively.
  • Cohesion – Conjunction Frequency: Total conjunction count with breakdown by type: Coordinating, Subordinating, Correlative, and Conjunctive Adverbs.
